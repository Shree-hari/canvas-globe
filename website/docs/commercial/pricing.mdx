# Simple licensing, no runtime lock-in

Use the complete library under GPLv3 when your project can comply. Buy a
commercial license when you need to keep a distributed product proprietary.
Every plan uses the same package and features.

| Plan | Price | Best for | Included scope |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Open Source** | **$0** | GPL-compatible projects | GPLv3; community support |
| **Solo Commercial** | **$79 perpetual** | One developer shipping one proprietary product | 1 developer, 1 product, 12 months of updates and standard support |
| **Team Commercial** | **$249 perpetual** | A small product team | Up to 5 developers, 5 products, 12 months of updates and standard support |
| **Business Commercial** | **$599 perpetual** | A larger team with several internal products | Up to 20 developers, unlimited internal products, 12 months of updates and priority support |
| **OEM / Builder** | **From $1,500/product/year** | SDKs, builders, templates, white-label, or generated copies | Negotiated redistribution rights |
| **Enterprise** | **Custom** | Affiliates, procurement, security terms, or larger scope | Negotiated agreement and support |

## Choose a plan

### Open Source

Use `licenseKey: "GPL-3.0"` and comply with GNU GPLv3. The GPL permits
commercial activity; the relevant issue is whether your copying, combination,
modification, and distribution comply with its terms.

## What “perpetual” means

You may keep using the versions delivered during the included 12-month update
period. Updates and support after that period require an optional renewal.
Final rights are governed by the commercial agreement attached to your order.

## Pre-launch notice

Checkout remains disabled until the commercial agreement and payment-provider
account have final approval. This avoids taking money under incomplete terms.
See the [commercial FAQ](/commercial-faq) or [launch playbook](https://github.com/swiftools/canvas-globe/blob/main/LAUNCH_PLAYBOOK.md).
