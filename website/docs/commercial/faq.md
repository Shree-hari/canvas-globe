# Commercial licensing FAQ

## Does every business need to pay?

No. GPLv3 permits commercial use. A commercial license is for teams that want
the proprietary permissions stated in the commercial agreement and order.

## Is this a separate “Pro” package?

No. npm, GPL, and commercial customers use the same package and API.

## What is a developer?

A developer is a person who accesses CanvasGlobe to develop or modify a covered
product. Employees and contractors both count. Final wording is controlled by
the agreement delivered with the order.

## What is a product?

A product is a website, application, service, or other offering covered by the
order. Deployments and environments of that same product do not normally count
as separate products. Reusable builders, SDKs, templates, white-label systems,
and generated copies require OEM terms.

## Can contractors use our license?

They can be covered only while working on your product, within the purchased
developer limit, and subject to the final agreement. You remain responsible
for their compliance.

## Does the license expire?

Perpetual plans permit continued use of versions delivered during the first 12
months. Update and support access after that period is an optional renewal.
OEM and Enterprise may use annual or negotiated terms.

## Can we redistribute CanvasGlobe?

Not under Solo, Team, or ordinary Business terms. Contact sales for SDKs,
builders, themes/templates, component libraries, white-label offerings,
installable products, and other downstream distribution.

## Can we evaluate before purchase?

Yes. Evaluate the full library. Do not ship a proprietary distribution until
you have GPL-compatible terms or a completed commercial order.

## Can we upgrade?

The intended policy is to credit the original order toward a larger plan
during its update period. This must be confirmed in the final checkout terms.

## Where is the legal agreement?

The approved agreement will be attached at checkout and to the order record.
Public pages are summaries; the accepted agreement and order control.
