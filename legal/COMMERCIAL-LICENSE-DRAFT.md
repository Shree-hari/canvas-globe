## 1. Definitions

- **Software** means the CanvasGlobe source code, compiled artifacts,
  documentation, and official updates supplied under an order.
- **Developer** means an employee or contractor who accesses the Software to
  develop or modify a Customer Product.
- **Customer Product** means a website, application, service, or other product
  identified or permitted by the purchased plan.
- **Redistributable Product** means a builder, SDK, template, component
  library, white-label product, installable product, or system that supplies
  the Software or generated copies to third parties.
- **Order Term** means the scope, seats, products, fees, support, and update
  period shown on the order form.

## 2. Commercial license grant

Subject to payment and compliance with this Agreement, Licensor grants
Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide license during the
applicable Order Term to use, reproduce, modify, and incorporate the Software
into permitted Customer Products, and to distribute those Customer Products
without applying GPLv3 to Customer's proprietary code.

Perpetual orders permit continued use and distribution of the versions
received during the update period. Access to later versions, support, and new
development rights after the included period are governed by the order form.

## 3. Scope by plan

- **Solo:** one Developer and one Customer Product.
- **Team:** up to five Developers and five Customer Products.
- **Business:** up to twenty Developers and unlimited internal Customer
  Products owned and controlled by one legal entity.
- **OEM / Builder:** only the Redistributable Product named in the order.
- **Enterprise:** the scope stated in the negotiated order, including any
  affiliates.

Customer may reassign a seat after a person permanently stops working on a
Customer Product, but may not rotate seats to avoid the purchased limit.

## 4. Restrictions

Unless an OEM / Builder or Enterprise order expressly allows it, Customer may
not:

- sell, sublicense, publish, or distribute the Software on a standalone basis;
- use the Software in a Redistributable Product;
- make the Software's source available outside Customer and its authorized
  contractors;
- remove copyright, license, or third-party notices;
- use a license key outside the purchased Customer entity and scope; or
- represent the Software or CanvasGlobe name as Customer's own library.

Customer may make reasonable internal backup copies and permit contractors to
use the Software solely for Customer, provided Customer remains responsible
for their compliance.
