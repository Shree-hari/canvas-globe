## License activation

When you activate or validate a CanvasGlobe commercial license, we
process the license key, license plan, activation identifier, project label,
activation and expiration dates, and basic request security information. We use
this information to provide the license, enforce activation limits, prevent
fraud, troubleshoot activation and maintain purchase records.

License activation occurs during developer setup or an application build. The
CanvasGlobe library does not use license activation to track visitors to a
customer's website and does not send globe interaction data to Swiftools.

Kelviq processes purchase, customer and license information as the checkout and
merchant-of-record provider. Cloudflare processes requests as the hosting and
security provider. Their respective privacy terms apply to their processing.

We retain activation and transaction records for as long as reasonably needed
to provide perpetual-license evidence, prevent abuse, meet tax and accounting
requirements, resolve disputes and comply with law. Support requests are
retained as needed to provide support and maintain service history.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact globe@swiftools.com. Some
records may be retained where required for legal, security, accounting or
license-verification purposes.
