# CanvasGlobe community standards

CanvasGlobe welcomes respectful technical discussion from people of every
background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual-property rights.
- Give credit for work and disclose relevant third-party material.
- Help keep issues, discussions, and reviews useful to future readers.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, impersonation,
publication of private information, and other abusive conduct are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report community-conduct concerns privately to
[globe@swiftools.com](mailto:globe@swiftools.com). Do not use a public issue for
sensitive reports. Harsh Jhunjhunuwala, as project owner, may edit or remove
content and restrict participation when reasonably necessary to protect the
community and project.

This document governs participation in CanvasGlobe project spaces. It does not
alter the software or commercial license terms.
