What I deliberately left out

Volumetric clouds, real atmospheric scattering, ray-traced glass and true depth-of-field all need a GPU. Anything here that imitates them does it with compositing tricks, which is honest at these canvas sizes but would fall apart at 4K. If you want any of those properly, that is a WebGL renderer, not an effect.
